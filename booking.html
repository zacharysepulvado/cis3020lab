Booking Process

  1. Choose your session above and open its live Square availability.
  2. Select an available date and time, then pay the required 50% deposit through Square.
  3. Once the deposit is successfully paid, your booking is automatically confirmed and the appointment time is reserved.
  4. Enjoy your session. Afterward, I send a preview gallery so you can select favorites.
  5. Pay the remaining 50% through Square before final edited gallery delivery.
  6. Receive your final edited images through a private online gallery.

Event and rush inquiries are reviewed separately and require my approval before a session can be arranged.

Rush Session Requests

Need a session inside the normal 14-day booking window? Send an inquiry. Rush sessions depend on my availability and approval.

  • 14+ days: normal pricing; book online through Square.
  • 7–13 days: may be accepted with a $50 rush fee.
  • 2–6 days: may be accepted with a $100 rush fee.
  • Less than 48 hours: not normally accepted; contact me to discuss whether I can accommodate your request.

An inquiry does not create an appointment or reserve a date.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a session?

Choose a fixed-price session above, view live availability in Square, and select a date and time. Your appointment is automatically confirmed and reserved once the required 50% deposit is successfully paid. Event photography and rush sessions require an inquiry and my approval.

How much is the deposit?

A 50% deposit is required when booking online. Square records it toward your total session price; the remaining 50% is due later. Full prepayment is not required.

When is the remaining balance due?

The remaining 50% is collected later through Square, before final gallery delivery.

When can I book, and where can I see availability?

Square shows live availability 14–90 days ahead. Photography is primarily offered Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays around seasonal golden-hour lighting; Monday–Thursday are not normally available. Select a session above to see the current dates and times.

Can I request a session with less than 14 days’ notice?

Yes, you can send a rush inquiry. Requests 7–13 days ahead may be accepted with a $50 rush fee; 2–6 days ahead may be accepted with a $100 rush fee. Less than 48 hours is not normally accepted. All rush requests depend on my availability and approval and do not automatically reserve an appointment.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

The 50% booking deposit is non-refundable. With at least 72 hours’ notice, it may be transferred one time to a rescheduled date. With less than 72 hours’ notice, the deposit is forfeited. A new 50% deposit is required to reserve another date after a late cancellation or reschedule.

How long does it take to receive the photos?

Most sessions have a turnaround time of 2 weeks. Event photography has a turnaround time of 3 weeks.

How are photos delivered?

Final edited photos are delivered through a private online gallery with full-resolution download access.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel is available depending on the location. Additional travel fees may apply outside of the local area.

Can I choose my favorite photos?

Yes. After the session, I send a preview gallery where clients can like and select favorite images before final editing.
